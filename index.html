About Me

Bridging Administration & Human-Centered Care

I am a healthcare administrator with a passion for building organizations where clinical excellence and compassionate culture go hand in hand. My work is grounded in operational strategy, team development, and evidence-based leadership.

Currently pursuing my Doctorate in Healthcare Administration (DHA), I bring academic rigor to the practical challenges healthcare leaders face — from workforce engagement and patient outcomes to regulatory compliance and system design.

I believe that emotionally intelligent leadership is the cornerstone of high-performing healthcare organizations, and I am committed to developing the next generation of leaders who lead with both head and heart.
